    What this page can and can't do

    • To stop a site now, open it and sign out there. That removes what it was given, at once.
    • Everything here also ends by its date, and when you clear this browser's data.
    • This page can't switch a site off from here, and can't see how often a site signed. A browser keeps what a site was given in storage that only that site's own pages can reach, and no page of BitLogin's can look into it.
    • "Remove from this list" takes a line off this page. It does not stop the site.
    • The list is kept in this browser only. Another browser or device has its own.
