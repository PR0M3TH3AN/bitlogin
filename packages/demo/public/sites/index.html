      How taking a site back works

      • "Take it back" is not instant. It publishes a note, sealed so only you can read it, on your relays. The site stops when its signer next reads that note: usually within a day, and three days at most if your relays can't be reached.
      • To stop a site this minute, open it and sign out there.
      • You can allow a site again afterwards. Nothing has to be undone.
      • This page can't see how often a site signed. A browser keeps what a site was given in storage that only that site's own pages can reach.
      • The list is kept in this browser only. Another browser or device has its own.
      • You need to be signed in here, and unlocked, to take a site back.
