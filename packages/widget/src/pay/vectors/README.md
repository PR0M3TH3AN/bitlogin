# BitUnlock's published test vectors

Fetched unchanged from `https://api.bitunlock.network/test-vectors/` on
2026-11-21. They are BitUnlock's published contract for two of the things a buyer's
side reads: a product listing, and a receipt's words. The payment plan and
delivery vectors were not served there on that day, so they are not here. `vectors.test.ts` runs BitLogin's buyer side against them.

Do not edit them. To take a newer set, fetch them again and change the date.
